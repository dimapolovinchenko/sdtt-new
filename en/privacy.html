Privacy policy

This English version is provided for information only. The German version is legally binding.

The protection of your personal data is important to us. Below we explain which data is processed when you visit this website and which rights you have.

1. Controller

SDTT GmbH
Eichendorffstraße 3
40474 Düsseldorf
Managing director: Shalva Papismedov
Email: info@sdtt.de
Phone: +49 176 74906009

2. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA. When you visit the website, the hosting provider processes technically necessary data (IP address, date and time of the request, page requested, amount of data transferred, browser type and operating system) in order to deliver the website and ensure its security. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure and efficient operation of the website). A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place. Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the EU standard contractual clauses. Log files are stored only for a short time and then deleted.

3. No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, marketing or tracking services. Fonts and all other content are loaded from our own server; no connection to other providers' servers is made. Only your privacy choice is stored in your browser as technically necessary storage (local storage, entry “sdtt-consent”) for six months (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Optional categories (statistics, external content) are prepared but currently not in use and stay off without your consent. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via the “Cookie settings” link in the footer.

4. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we process your details (e.g. name, contact details and the content of your enquiry) solely to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if the enquiry relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The website has no contact form. The WhatsApp link only takes you to that service after you click it; the privacy policy of WhatsApp Ireland Ltd. then applies, and data may be transferred to third countries.

5. Downloads

The documents offered (e.g. project archive, certificates) can be downloaded without registration. Only the technical data described in section 2 is processed.

6. Storage period

We delete personal data as soon as the purpose of processing no longer applies, unless statutory retention obligations (e.g. under the German Commercial Code or Fiscal Code) require otherwise.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). To exercise your rights, an informal message to the address above is sufficient.

8. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia (LDI NRW), Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany.

9. Security

For security reasons this website uses encrypted transmission (TLS/HTTPS).
