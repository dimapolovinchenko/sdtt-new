Our commitments for public projects

Binding standards we meet in every contract with public clients.

Requirement 1

VOB/DIN compliance

  • All works carried out according to VOB/B, VOB/C and the relevant DIN standards
  • Unrestricted access for the client's site supervision
  • Progress reports at least weekly
  • Complete documentation: as-built drawings, material certificates, test reports
  • Contractual penalties for delays as set out in the tender (§ 11 VOB/B)
Requirement 2

Quality management

  • Processes according to ISO 9001:2015
  • Acceptance supported by independent inspectors (TÜV or DEKRA)
  • At least two internal or external audits per month
  • Certificates for all materials (CE marking, test certificates)
  • Liability for defects of up to 5 years under § 634a BGB
Requirement 3

Site safety

  • Safety coordination according to DGUV Rule 100-001 and the Construction Site Ordinance
  • Annual documented safety training for all employees
  • Emergency plan for every site
  • Accident and incident log, open to the client
  • Zero-accident target with documented risk assessment
Requirement 4

Cost control

  • Detailed cost breakdown 30 days before construction starts
  • Change orders only after written instruction by the client
  • Weekly budget-versus-actual cost report
  • No hidden costs
  • Deviation of no more than ±5% from the initial estimate
