Standards & certificates

The standards we build to for public clients – with proof available for download.

ISO 9001:2015

Quality management system

Certified quality management

Our quality management system is certified to ISO 9001:2015 and audited annually by an independent certification body.

What this means for you

  • Documented processes from estimating to acceptance
  • Regular internal audits and management reviews
  • Full traceability of materials and inspections

Certificates ISO 9001, VOB/DIN (PDF)

VOB/B · VOB/C · DIN

German construction contract procedures

VOB/DIN compliance

We carry out all works according to VOB/B (contract conditions) and VOB/C (general technical specifications, ATV) and the relevant DIN standards.

What this means for you

  • Transparent invoicing based on measured quantities
  • Written notices of concerns and hindrances under § 4 and § 6 VOB/B
  • Formal acceptance under § 12 VOB/B

Project management methodology (PDF)

GEG

Buildings Energy Act

Energy requirements

We carry out energy refurbishments and new builds according to the Buildings Energy Act and document the proof for the client.

What this means for you

  • Execution according to the energy performance calculation and specialist design
  • Airtightness testing on request
  • Contractor declarations under the GEG

DGUV Regel 100-001

Principles of prevention

Occupational safety on site

We organise occupational safety according to DGUV Rule 100-001, the Occupational Safety and Health Act (ArbSchG), the Workplace Ordinance (ArbStättV) and the Construction Site Ordinance.

What this means for you

  • Risk assessment and emergency plan for every site
  • Annual documented safety training
  • Accident and incident log open to the client

§ 634a BGB

Limitation of claims for defects

5-year warranty

For buildings we are liable for defects for five years from acceptance. For VOB/B contracts we also agree five years on request.

What this means for you

  • Defects remedied by our own staff
  • Warranty bond on request
  • Inspection before the warranty period expires

Handwerkskammer

Stuttgart Region Chamber of Crafts

Entry in the trade register

SDTT GmbH is entered in the trade register of the Stuttgart Region Chamber of Crafts. The registration number is listed in the imprint.

What this means for you

  • Master craftsman business in the registered trades
  • Training company

Our commitments in every contract

Requirement 1

VOB/DIN compliance

  • All works carried out according to VOB/B, VOB/C and the relevant DIN standards
  • Unrestricted access for the client's site supervision
  • Progress reports at least weekly
  • Complete documentation: as-built drawings, material certificates, test reports
  • Contractual penalties for delays as set out in the tender (§ 11 VOB/B)
Requirement 2

Quality management

  • Processes according to ISO 9001:2015
  • Acceptance supported by independent inspectors (TÜV or DEKRA)
  • At least two internal or external audits per month
  • Certificates for all materials (CE marking, test certificates)
  • Liability for defects of up to 5 years under § 634a BGB
Requirement 3

Site safety

  • Safety coordination according to DGUV Rule 100-001 and the Construction Site Ordinance
  • Annual documented safety training for all employees
  • Emergency plan for every site
  • Accident and incident log, open to the client
  • Zero-accident target with documented risk assessment
Requirement 4

Cost control

  • Detailed cost breakdown 30 days before construction starts
  • Change orders only after written instruction by the client
  • Weekly budget-versus-actual cost report
  • No hidden costs
  • Deviation of no more than ±5% from the initial estimate
